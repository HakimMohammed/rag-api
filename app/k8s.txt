Kubernetes (K8s) is an open-source platform for automating the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications, treating containers like an operating system for the cloud to simplify running complex, distributed apps. It handles tasks like load balancing, self-healing (restarting failed containers), and rolling out updates, allowing developers to build "build-once-deploy-anywhere" cloud-native apps, originally developed by Google and now maintained by the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF).
